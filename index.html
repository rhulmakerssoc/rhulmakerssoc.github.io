What We Do

Explore, Learn, and Create Together

From competitive robotics tournaments to creative Lego socials and electronics workshops, we provide the tools, community, and space to bring your ideas to life!

Workshops

Many of the following are covered in our workshops:

  • Soldering
  • Embedded Systems Programming
  • Robotics / Control Engineering
  • CAD & CAM – 3D & 2D Modelling, 3D Printing & Laser Cutting
3D Printing & CAD

Learn CAD modeling, slicing software, and fabricate custom components using the society 3D printer and EE laser cutters.

Access Hours

As members, you can access the SHILLING-1-06 electronics lab during our set access hours, as long as there is staff & committee present!

Fabricating My Designs

Become a member, email us the 3D model as an STL at makers@royalholloway.su & we will work with you to establish the needs of the file.

In some cases, the file may be too large for our printer, in both materials or time. In these cases, a "Large Print Charge" may need to be bought for us to print it.

Southern Universities Robotics League (SURL)

Founded right here at Royal Holloway, SURL brings university maker societies together for combat robotics showdowns, rover challenges, and technical engineering exhibitions.
